# Crypto Security Incidents Covered by The Crypto Time (August to November 2026)

Seven crypto security incidents reported by The Crypto Time between August and October 2026: exchange hacks, DeFi lending exploits, a sidechain exploit and hardware wallet attacks. Each row gives the loss figure as stated in our article and the latest figure reported by an outside source, checked on 24 November 2026.

## Files
- `tct-crypto-security-incidents-2026-11-24.csv`: one row per incident
- `tct-crypto-security-incidents-2026-11-24.json`: same data as JSON

## Columns
- `incident_id`, `incident`, `affected_entity`, `incident_date` (blank when the date is not stated), `incident_type`, `chain_or_product`
- `our_article_loss_usd`: a number only when our article states one loss figure; otherwise blank, with the wording in `our_article_loss_note`
- `latest_reported_loss_usd`, `latest_reported_note`, `latest_source_url`: the most recent outside estimate found on 24 November 2026
- `our_article_url`, `our_article_published`, `article_source_urls`: our coverage and the sources it cites

## Notes
- Two figures changed after our articles were published. Binance: our article gave an unconfirmed estimate of 5.3 to 5.9 billion USD; Binance later put the impact at about 6.3 billion USD. Tectonic on Cronos: our article gave about 75 million USD; Bitquery later revised it to 120.4 million USD, and validators rolled the chain back.
- Ledger: no confirmed total loss. Outside reports say losses may exceed 86 million USD.
- Loss estimates are as reported, not audited. Most are early estimates and may change again.

## Method
Figures in our articles were checked against the article text. Latest figures were checked against the linked outside source on 24 November 2026.
